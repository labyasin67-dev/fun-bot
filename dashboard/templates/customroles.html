Custom Roles

Members at or above the minimum level can create one custom role. You can turn the feature off, set the level, control whether members can give their role to someone else, and restrict anyone who abuses it. Staff can also set or edit a role's colours and gradients below.

{% if not gradients_supported %}

This server doesn't have enhanced role colours (extra boosts), so Discord will only use the first colour. A second hex is still saved here and will apply if the server later gets gradients.

{% endif %}

Settings

New custom roles are placed just below the bot's highest role, or just below the anchor role if you pick one. They have no permissions and never ping. When .role give is on, a member can hand their custom role to someone else, including members who already own a custom role. A member can hold their own role plus roles given to them. The same role can't be given twice. The owner can take it back with .role take. Staff with Manage Roles can give any custom role, even if this toggle is off.

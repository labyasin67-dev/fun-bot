Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Information we collect

Fun bot ("we", "us", "our") is a Discord bot. We collect only the data necessary to operate the bot and its dashboard:

  • Discord user IDs — to identify users and servers the bot is in.
  • Guild (server) IDs — to scope settings per server.
  • Channel IDs — for features like welcome messages, reports tickets, and game announcements.
  • Role IDs — for custom roles and self-roles.
  • Message content — temporarily processed in-memory for game responses and moderation; not stored permanently.
  • OAuth tokens — Discord and YouTube OAuth access/refresh tokens stored encrypted to authenticate API calls on your behalf.

2. How we use your data

We use collected data solely to operate and improve the bot:

  • Running drop-in games, leveling, and XP tracking.
  • Sending welcome messages, reports, and moderation actions.
  • Managing custom roles and self-roles.
  • Posting scheduled YouTube videos (when you authorize YouTube access).
  • Rendering the dashboard.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties except:

  • Discord — via the Discord API to operate the bot.
  • Google/YouTube — only when you explicitly authorize via `/youtube auth`, solely to post videos on your behalf.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect safety.

4. Data retention

  • Game and XP data is retained while the bot is in your server; it is deleted when the bot leaves or is removed.
  • OAuth tokens are stored until you revoke them or remove the bot.
  • Report tickets and moderation logs are retained until deleted by server staff.

5. Your rights

You may:

  • Request deletion of your data by removing the bot from your server or contacting us.
  • Revoke OAuth access at any time via your Discord or Google account settings.
  • Contact us with questions about your data.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect data, including encrypted storage of OAuth tokens and restricted access to production systems.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the bot after changes constitute acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact the bot owner through the Discord server or support channel listed on the bot's profile.

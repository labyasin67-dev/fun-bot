Reports

Members can report a message or a person with .report or the Report Message right-click menu. By default, reports are posted as an embed in your tickets channel. A private ticket channel is optional.

When this is off, .report tells members that reports are disabled. New reports go to the tickets channel (or a ticket under the category, in ticket mode). When a report is approved, denied or closed, a summary is posted to the closed tickets channel and the original is removed. Only the staff role is pinged for new reports. Decision reasons are sent to the reporter by DM.
